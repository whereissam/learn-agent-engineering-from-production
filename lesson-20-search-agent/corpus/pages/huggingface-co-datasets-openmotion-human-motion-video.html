openmotion/human-motion-video - a dataset of human motion clips

Published 2026-02-11 · huggingface.co

A dataset of 12,400 monocular video clips of everyday human motion, each with a fitted SMPL sequence. Intended for training video-to-humanoid retargeting models.

Clips are 2 to 10 seconds, 30 fps, 1080p. Camera is static in 80 percent of clips.
